# M4 — Chinese research layer

M4 prepares a Chinese-language research layer for eligible M3 cases. It is not a finished article generator or a publishing integration.

## Stored output

- Chinese title
- 300–500 character Chinese synopsis
- Five-part story outline
- One Chinese rendering for every active indexed fact

Each Chinese fact is keyed to its English fact ID and source hash. English values and evidence remain authoritative.

## Idempotency and budgets

The source signature includes the original story hash, indexed fact IDs/values/evidence, model, and prompt version. Unchanged cases are skipped without model calls. The scheduled workflow processes at most five new or stale cases per run.

Invalid JSON, missing/duplicate fact IDs, or source-hash mismatches reject the entire case transaction. A failure does not modify the original English source record.
